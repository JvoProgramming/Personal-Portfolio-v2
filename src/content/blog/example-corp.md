My first year as a full-time engineer taught me that **shipping small** beats shipping big. A few lessons:

- Write the design doc, even if it's one page.
- Ask the "dumb" question in the meeting; three other people had it too.
- Leave the codebase a little better than you found it.

The biggest surprise was how much of the job is communication.
